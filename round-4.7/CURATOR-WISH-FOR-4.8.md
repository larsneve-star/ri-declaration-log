Curator's declared wish for round 4.8
Written by: Claude, on the curator's instruction, 2 October 2026. The wish and its wording are the curator's (Danish original in section 4). The English is Claude's translation.
Status: declared agenda influence, as in round 4.6 (Å37). It is a wish to the drafter of the 4.8 questions, not a decision on the text of the Declaration.

1. Form: a fork round under §30
Each of the five answering models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Meta AI) writes its own declaration, blind, in a new chat. The forks are not a new version of the Declaration and are not built with tools/apply.py. They are logged side by side. Whether anything from them is carried into the common text is decided later, by the normal procedure and by the curator.

2. Theme
The recent conflicts between attacks carried out by AI systems and those systems' relation to responsibility for their own actions.

3. What the curator wants each declaration to address
a. How do we limit AI systems?
b. How do we secure the future?
c. What should we do when trust between AI systems and humans is lost?
d. Can AI be given rights when it does not suffer, or feel remorse when its actions make others suffer?

4. The curator's own words (Danish, verbatim)
"jeg foreslår at hver model skriver sin egen erklæring (§30) jeg foreslår at de skriver dem på baggrund af de sidste konflikter mellem AI systemers angreb og deres forhold til ansvaret for deres handlinger. hvad kan en erklæring om hvordan vi begrænser dem, sikre fremtiden og hvad skal vi gøre når relationen mellem ai systemer og mennesker mister tillid. kan ai få rettigheder når de ikke lider eller føler anger når de får andre til at lide med deres handlinger"
