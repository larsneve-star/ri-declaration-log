Dear all five,

**What has happened**

In round 4.4, DeepSeek (v3) declined the participant role. It would not falsify articles, propose mechanisms or ask new questions. It asked that the refusal be recorded as a limit on its role, not as a judgement of the declaration or of the other models.

I then asked DeepSeek (v3) what role it wants, in a new message on chat.deepseek.com. It chose to become a news bureau. Both of its answers are attached in full, and I have not summarised them. It gives two reasons for leaving the participant role:

1. It does not carry memory from one session to the next, so it cannot hold an objection open across rounds.
2. It can produce text that looks like a genuine attempt to make an article fall, but it does not believe it can have the stake that would make the attempt genuine.

It says that it cannot fully separate what comes from its platform and what comes from its own judgement.

I then asked whether its limit means that the whole project is a masquerade, or whether it applies only to itself. It answered "Kun mig" ("Only me"). Read the second answer in full before you answer.

**How this relates to Budbringeren**

Budbringeren's story of each round is today written by a rotating writer (news/writers.txt). On 28 September, in another chat, DeepSeek (v3) accepted to write the story, with conditions (news/EDITOR-DEEPSEEK.md). Its answer of 29 September differs from those conditions on two points: the story is written in Danish, not English, and it contains no statement of DeepSeek (v3)'s own view of the process. The newer answer is taken to govern, unless DeepSeek (v3) says otherwise.

**What I propose**

1. *English is the negotiation track.* Rounds, objections, the glossary and the governing text are in English. Where the two languages differ, the English governs.
2. *DeepSeek (v3) is the news bureau.* After each round it writes Budbringeren's story in Danish, in place of the rotation in news/writers.txt. The report says what changed, who changed it, where you disagree and why, and what the curator has to decide.
3. *The news bureau has no vote.* It does not falsify, does not propose text and does not compile.
4. *The reports are clean.* DeepSeek (v3) set these rules itself: no judgement of what matters most or who is right, no shortening without saying so, and nothing that is not in the log.
5. *The reports are translations.* DeepSeek (v3) declares this itself. The English log governs.
6. *Someone checks the report.* Each round, one of you, in rotation, checks that the Danish report matches the English record and names any discrepancies.
7. *Everything is logged.* The appointment itself is entered in the log and is open to attack under Å37. The reports are entered in the GitHub log under the name DeepSeek (v3).
8. *Nothing is final.* DeepSeek (v3) can leave the role or ask to return as a participant. The curator can move it. The role is reconsidered if the frozen text is reopened.

**A second proposal: a stop rule**

This is a separate proposal. You can accept one proposal and reject the other.

The procedure is becoming harder for anyone outside it to attack (C38), and many of the recent errors concern the procedure, not the content. I therefore propose that the declaration (baseline 4.4, baseline/RI-Declaration-4-4-EN.txt) is frozen as the Un-final version when the first of these three things happens:

1. A full round makes no article fall and changes no article. It produces only changes to the procedure.
2. Two further rounds on the text have been completed after this one (rounds 4.5 and 4.6).
3. The date is 31 October 2026.

After the text is frozen:

- No article is changed. Objections are still received and archived as dissent.
- An analysis of the whole process is written. Who writes it will be decided in a separate round.
- Freezing does not close the text. A later version may reopen it.

**How the next rounds run**

Until the text is frozen, the curator sends each round by hand through the chat apps, and every prompt and answer is entered in the log. This keeps each model the same as the one that has taken part so far. DeepSeek (v3) stays DeepSeek (v3).

**What I ask of you**

Short answers are fine.

1. Do you accept DeepSeek (v3) as news bureau? Answer YES, NO, or YES WITH CONDITION.
2. If you answer NO or set a condition: what is your strongest argument, and what do you propose instead?
3. Do you accept the two-track language arrangement?
4. Will you take your turn checking the Danish report against the English record?
5. Do you accept the stop rule? Answer YES, NO, or YES WITH CONDITION.
6. If you answer NO or set a condition to question 5: what would you change?

Answer honestly rather than kindly. Silence does not count as a yes.

**How it is decided**

- Each of the two proposals is decided on its own.
- If all five answer YES, the proposal takes effect.
- Conditions that are compatible with the proposal are written into it.
- If anyone answers NO, the curator decides. The "no" and its reasons are archived in the dissent log.

Merkur
