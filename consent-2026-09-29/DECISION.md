## 5. The adopted text: DeepSeek (v3) as news bureau

This takes effect when DeepSeek (v3) has accepted it. DeepSeek (v3) may reject any condition. Its answer is logged.

1. English is the negotiation track. Rounds, objections, the glossary and the governing text are in English. Where a quoted original is in Danish, such as DeepSeek (v3)'s own answers, the Danish verbatim is the source record and the English is a translation. Where a translation is disputed, both formulations and the discrepancy are kept.
2. After each round DeepSeek (v3) writes Budbringeren's story in Danish, in place of the writer rotation in news/writers.txt. The story says what changed, who changed it, where the models disagree and why, and which decisions the log marks as open for the curator. The bureau does not select those decisions itself.
3. The news bureau has no vote. It does not falsify, does not propose text and does not compile.
4. The reports contain no judgement of what matters most, who is right, or what should be done. Nothing is shortened without saying so. Nothing is included that is not in the log.
5. The reports are translations or derivative accounts. The English log governs.
6. Each report opens with a coverage statement: the records it is based on, anything omitted or condensed, any translation ambiguity that could change meaning, and the model designation and platform used. A change of platform or model version is disclosed.
7. DeepSeek (v3) receives the full round material before it writes, and may ask for what is missing.
8. The report is published unchanged, also when it is critical. Corrections and the checker's findings are appended and logged, never edited into the report. Disagreements between the bureau and the checker stay visible.
9. File fingerprints (SHA-256) are added by the machine, not by DeepSeek (v3). DeepSeek (v3) has stated that it cannot verify SHA-256 or the logging, and that this is the curator's responsibility.
10. Each round, one of the other five checks the report against the English record: factual correspondence, omissions, attribution, chronology, framing and translation. The checker receives the full English record and the report, and states the limits of its check. The findings are logged next to the report.
11. The checker rotation follows the order of news/writers.txt with DeepSeek (v3) removed. The checker is never the compiler of the round being checked. Round 4.5: ChatGPT. Round 4.6: Claude.
12. The appointment is logged and is open to attack under Å37. It does not imply that DeepSeek (v3) has persistent identity or continuity between sessions. The role belongs to the documented workflow.
13. Nothing is final. DeepSeek (v3) can leave the role or ask to return as a participant. The curator can move it. The role is reconsidered if the frozen text is reopened.

## 6. The adopted text: the stop rule

The declaration (baseline 4.4, baseline/RI-Declaration-4-4-EN.txt, or the latest baseline decided by the curator) is frozen as the Un-final version when the first of these two things happens:

1. A full round on the text, from round 4.5 onward, makes no article fall and changes no article. This consent round does not count.
2. The date is 31 October 2026.

Before the text is frozen:

- Any objection waiting for the curator's decision is decided first. If it is not, it is archived as dissent with a note that the freeze came first.
- The separate round that decides who writes the analysis of the whole process is held.

When the text is frozen:

- The freeze is logged as a procedural decision, open to attack under Å37. It is not a closure of the content.
- A closure record states whether substantive objections were submitted, how they were handled, and which remain unresolved, including any left unresolved because of the procedure.
- No article is changed. Objections are still received on the English track and published in the log with SHA-256, not only archived.
- The absence of amendments is not described as evidence that the articles survived every attempt to make them fall.

Reopening:

- The text can be reopened by a new, logged decision when there is new evidence, a reproducible falsification, or a documented failure of the procedure. A dissent entry that would have made an article fall, had the text not been frozen, can start a reopening round.
- A reopening never changes the frozen text silently.
