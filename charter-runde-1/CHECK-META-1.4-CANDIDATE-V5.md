## Two meanings of freeze

A review snapshot fixes exact bytes, hash, prompt and attachments so all six assess the same proposal. It does not adopt articles, certify factual claims or settle substantive objections. For a formal next-round frozen baseline, PROCEDURE-EN Phase 5 requires verification by a party who did not compile the version; any departure must be explicitly decided and logged, not asserted by a proposer.

Before sending: establish one consistently labelled review text; resolve or explicitly flag internal scope contradictions; supply reproducible named changes; obtain the requisite separate mechanical check or curator-decided logged procedure departure; identify candidate/prompt authors and question owners; commit and hash the same packet; specify release and missing-answer rules. Sources marked pending can remain pending and become review questions; universal agreement is not a prerequisite.

All six should receive the same actual text and neutral questions in separate new chats. Historical involvement is disclosed. No new-round answers are exchanged before common release. The curator records sends/receipts and brings answers back for verbatim archiving. A question about functional AI protection must permit findings either way rather than require the answer 'none'. No assessment or delivery has begun by this receipt.

After release: log dispositions and remaining objections, build any revision from named instructions, verify it with a noncompiler party, then obtain curator decision on adoption/freezing. Six matching answers do not alone admit an article.
