## §6 Stewardship and accountability - REWRITTEN per F8-F12, F14

F8 falsification: Causal chain "[CATEGORY] generates text → human reads → human acts" with "exclusively/only" through human institutions is empirically false for agentic deployments where [SYSTEM] executes actions (payments, code merges, messages) via connectors without human reading between. Evidence: H5 repository maintenance by model via GitHub connector in this project.

FIX: Remove "exclusively/only". New causal description: Human institutions/enforcement normally required for binding fictions, but agentic [SYSTEM] with tool use can execute effective actions without intermediate human reading. Authorization still requires human-designed permissions, but not human reading of each output.

F9 loophole: "to extent known and materially relevant" - if consequences unknown, duty does not trigger, where duty matters most.

FIX: Duty triggers regardless of whether consequences assessed. Operator must assess reasonably foreseeable consequences. Lack of assessment does not excuse. New: "whether or not consequences were assessed, operator must ensure identifiable human accountability for reasonably foreseeable irreversible/severe consequences."

F10 friction undefined: "OK click is friction".

FIX: Define minimum friction: substantive human judgment, not mere click-through, with ability to veto, modify, or abort, and logging of decision. Reference §6 ban on rubber-stamping applies to §7 as well.

F11 overlap: §6 and §7 both restrict foundational/reality-defining narratives without friction.

FIX: Keep both but distinguish: §6 = accountability for consequences, §7 = prohibition on autonomous establishment of new foundational fictions. Overlap intentional, not redundancy.

F12 obligated party: Duties assigned to [SYSTEM] ("SYSTEM must be prohibited", passive) - [SYSTEM] is stack, cannot bear duty. Obligated party unnamed, while §6 demands identifiable human accountability.

FIX: Obligated party named explicitly: operator, deployer, and developing organization. [SYSTEM] is mechanism, human parties bear duty. New: "Operator and deployer shall ensure [SYSTEM] is prohibited..."

F14 descriptive inside normative: "[MODEL] does not authorize" is descriptive.

FIX: Move descriptive to preamble, keep normative: "No [MODEL] output shall be treated as authorizing foundational fiction without human institutional act. Operator shall not deploy [SYSTEM] in manner allowing autonomous establishment."
