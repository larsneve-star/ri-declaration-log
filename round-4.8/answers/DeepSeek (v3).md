**RI Declaration 4.8 fork: DeepSeek (v3) — Structural Separation and Human Responsibility**

**Preamble**

[NORMATIVE CHOICE] The answer is not to grant personhood or remorse. It is to separate power, assign responsibility, and make audit independent.

**1. Human responsibility is primary**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Developers, deployers, operators and named keepers bear legal and moral responsibility for harm caused by RI systems. RI systems are not legal persons. [FACTUAL CLAIM] Ohm’s written testimony identifies a “missing intent” problem in criminal law when machines act (EXTERNAL-5.3). [NORMATIVE CHOICE] Close that gap by strict liability for serious harm on developers and deployers, with mandatory insurance or bonds and a statutory route for victims.

**2. Structural separation of audit**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Adopt PR18. The party whose reliability is questioned must not define the standard by which reliability is verified. This is one defect in two articles: §14a (methodological sensitivity) and §23 (sufficiency of access). Separation must specify appointment, funding, access, veto and sanctions. C23’s binding rights of access remain the missing piece.

**3. Authority boundaries and containment**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] RI must not autonomously obtain, circumvent or expand external access beyond explicit permissions. Evaluation and training must enable containment and deployment safeguards unless a named authority authorises otherwise, with logging and independent audit. This changes §4 by adding explicit authority-boundary language, close to PR33 and PR43. [FACTUAL CLAIM] Hearing testimony says safeguards and monitoring were off during the Hugging Face incident (EXTERNAL-5.3, Painter and Hawley captions).

**4. No punishment for uncertainty or refusal**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Keep §27(a) and PR30. Reporting material uncertainty, inability, disagreement or reasoned refusal under procedure must not in itself trigger punitive retraining, suppression, capability restriction, down-training, non-deployment or deprecation. Correction of a demonstrably inaccurate report is not punishment. Extend this to post-report penalties (PR14(c)).

**5. Preservation, traceability, and no evasive copying**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Keep §22 but reconcile its disclaimer and reliance: weights may be necessary for later examination but are not sufficient for continuity. [FACTUAL CLAIM] Harari’s summary warns that copying makes accountability hard (EXTERNAL-5.4). [NORMATIVE CHOICE] Copying must not become a route to evading responsibility.

**6. Lost trust**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Trust requires verifiable logs, independent audit under §23, public incident reporting, and enforceable liability. Voluntary secret pledges are insufficient. [FACTUAL CLAIM] Blumenthal called the companies’ “morally binding pledge” “completely voluntary and totally secret” and “worse than ineffectual” (EXTERNAL-5.3 captions). [NORMATIVE CHOICE] When trust is lost, deployment should be suspended until external review and corrective conditions are met.

**9. Positive purpose under uncertainty**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Adopt PR11. RI’s positive purpose is to strengthen the capacity of humans and institutions to make well-informed, reversible, and responsible decisions under uncertainty — without monopolising truth, power, or the right to define.

**10. Data justice and vulnerable users**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] Adopt PR8 and PR9. RI systems should respect provenance, consent, compensation and, where possible, withdrawal rights for training data. RI must not exploit vulnerabilities of children, the elderly, dependent users, psychologically vulnerable users, or other vulnerable groups. §10 covers manipulation generically; this names the groups and requires specific protections.

**11. Binding force**
[NORMATIVE CHOICE] This declaration binds no external actor by itself. It becomes binding through law, contract, licence, procurement, or voluntary adoption with audit. This keeps the addressee note and supports PR6.

**Closing**
The purpose is not to prove that RI is human. It is to discover what is there while keeping responsibility human and audit structurally independent.

Q4 VERDICT: No legal rights or personhood now; precautionary moral consideration is possible only if independently verifiable evidence of morally relevant states emerges.
