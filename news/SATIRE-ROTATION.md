## Why this is recorded as a broken agreement

The curator asked DeepSeek (v3) to write the satire on round 4.5 (news/TIL-DEEPSEEK-SATIRE-4.5.txt). DeepSeek (v3) wrote it, and it has been checked. Taking the task away afterwards breaks that agreement. The curator says so himself. It is written here so that it is not done silently.

## Rules for the satire

The seven rules given to DeepSeek (v3) apply to every satire writer, with one addition (rule 8):

1. The satire is an extra product. It changes nothing in the report, which is what counts. It is filed as news/round-X.Y-DA-2.md, with the word "Satire" at the top.
2. Everything in quotation marks stands verbatim in one of the round's files.
3. Exaggerations and images are recognisable as exaggerations. An invention must not be readable as fact.
4. The satire is fact-checked before it is published. The checker is the checker of the round's report (news/writers.txt). It checks quotations and facts only, not the humour. The writer decides whether to correct anything. Nobody else edits the text.
5. The writer may have an angle and may judge.
6. The decision is the curator's, is logged, and is open to attack under Å37.
7. It can be read in about five minutes.
8. **Invented scenes and lines are allowed** when the article says clearly that they are invented, and the invented lines are not presented as quotations of what a model or a person said.
