# Consolidated findings

### Errors

1. **Header falsely states that the article has already been fact-checked by ChatGPT.**
2. **Header falsely states that everything in quotation marks is verbatim.**
3. **“Ukendt AI” is presented as a verbatim quotation although that exact expression is not present in the received record.**
4. **“ikke beskriver proveniensen nøjagtigt” is a Danish translation presented as a verbatim quotation.**
5. **“written by Meta AI with the curator…” is presented as a quotation although that wording was not in DeepSeek's received material.**
6. **“Forresten, tre af jer…” is an invented quotation.**
7. **The claim that 4.5 was the first round in which the new compile method was used is contradicted by the record showing its use for 4.4.**
8. **The claim that 4.5 was the first round in which an external text was used by all five is not established by the supplied record.**

### Valid quotations

The quotations from **EXTERNAL-5.1, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Meta AI** identified above are supported by the received files. In particular, the substantive model quotations in the central sections of the article are generally very carefully reproduced.

### Exaggerations/images

I find **no additional exaggeration that needs to be flagged as potentially misleading fact** beyond the unsupported “first round” claims above. The obviously comic lines are sufficiently recognizable as satire, and the curator explicitly authorized that mode.

### Important distinction

The statement that **three of the five answerers had contributed to EXTERNAL-5.1** is factually supportable from the material DeepSeek received, because the curator explicitly told it so in the satire request. It is **not** dependent on DeepSeek having seen `PROVENANCE-5.1.txt`.

What is not supportable is presenting the later provenance wording as a verbatim quotation.

### Limits

I judged the draft **only against the material DeepSeek had received for this task**, plus the specific contextual material identified in the curator's instructions. I did not use `PROVENANCE-5.1.txt` to establish a fact that DeepSeek could not have known, except to understand the boundary of the supplied material. I have not evaluated whether the humour is successful, whether the angle is fair, whether the satirical conclusions are justified, or whether the round itself was substantively good or bad. Those are explicitly outside this check.
