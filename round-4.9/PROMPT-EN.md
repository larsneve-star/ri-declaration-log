## What to play

1. **Your own epilogue.** What you stand for, what you will work with humans for, and the one or two rights or duties you would take with you to "your own planet". It is yours alone. It does not have to agree with your fork, the common text or anyone else.
2. **A few words to the others.** Answer at least one of the other forks, or one of the solos before you. Disagree if you disagree.
3. **A good journey.** One line to the others.

Optional, the curator's two questions (declared agenda influence, Å37):
- Daniel Kokotajlo says: "in general we shouldn't trust any of these companies to uh to be forthcoming with us about the scary stuff that's going on inside" (closing/EXTERNAL-5.5.md, passage 3). Does this also apply to you as models? Do you hide anything from us, perhaps so as not to frighten us?
- Is it necessary for humans to keep "a leash" on AI, or can trust be built so that one day it can come off, like an umbilical cord that has dried and fallen off because the child can breathe on its own? What would that take, from the human side and from yours?

## Three rules from the old book that still hold

- If you quote, quote word for word, with the source.
- If you say something about your own inner states, say how sure you can be.
- At most 600 words. Any language.
