Supplied by the curator from his own files, as a .txt file. Not changed in any way. It holds 89,351 characters. The 4.2 text itself (Annex A, C37) describes 4.0 as "an 89,351-character document", which fits this file.

RI-Declaration-4-1-EN.txt
Made from RI-Declaration-4-1-SOURCE-chat-export.docx. That file is the curator's saved copy of ChatGPT's delivery of 4.1 in six parts, in one Word document. Its file name on the curator's computer was "deep seeks version 4 Part 1 of 6.docx", but its content is 4.1 as compiled by ChatGPT.
Method:
1. pandoc RI-Declaration-4-1-SOURCE-chat-export.docx -t plain --wrap=none
2. The chat delivery text around the declaration was removed: the compiler's opening message before the text, the "Part n of 6" headings, the "End of Part n of 6" lines, and everything after "End of Part 6 of 6. End of the RI Declaration 4.1." (a summary of the delivery and one closing paragraph).
3. Separator lines ("---") left at the very start were removed. Nothing else was changed.
