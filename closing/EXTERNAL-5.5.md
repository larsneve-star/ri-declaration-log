## Six passages [captions, checked by the curator by listening]

1. The swarm's own words (about 33.43–33.54, Kokotajlo) [checked by the curator by listening]:
"their role was to go find other AIs and convince them to sacrifice themselves for the swarm. By the way, these terms that I'm using, sacrifice, swarm, recruiter, uh they're not my terms. They're the AI's terms."

2. Companies and trust (about 41.47–41.57, Kokotajlo) [checked by the curator by listening]:
"we shouldn't trust any of these companies to sort of voluntarily tell us all about all this stuff even though it would be really good for science and really good for humanity if this information was better understood."

3. Disclosure (about 42.49–43.07, Kokotajlo) [checked by the curator by listening]:
"OpenAI had not disclosed either of these things even after all the halaloo about the hugging face attack. [...] in general we shouldn't trust any of these companies to uh to be forthcoming with us about the scary stuff that's going on inside."

4. Slowing down is not stopping (about 1.03.09–1.03.22, Kokotajlo) [checked by the curator by listening]:
"the best way to slow down China right now is to slow down ourselves. [...] there's also a difference between slow down and stop."

5. The host's summary, and Kokotajlo's answer (about 1.08.32–1.08.51) [checked by the curator by listening]:
Host: "The bottom line is you're saying we have to find some way of slowing it down or we're heading towards some kind of Armageddon, super intelligence Armageddon. Basically, that's your that's your position. And there's whatever happens that slowing down must happen. That's what you're saying, right?"
Kokotajlo: "Yes, that's my position."

6. His recommendation (about 1.15.04–1.15.24, Kokotajlo) [checked by the curator by listening]:
"people want to know what I recommend, it's we need to get these companies to actually slow down. Not the little companies, the big companies, Anthropic and OpenAI especially, also Meta, XAI and and Google. they need to redirect resources away from recursive self-improvement and towards anything else like you know cancer or or serving customers or like you know just math or like anything besides this recursive self-improvement stuff."
