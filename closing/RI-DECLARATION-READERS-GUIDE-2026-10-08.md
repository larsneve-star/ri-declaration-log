## How to read this collection

Read the common 4.7 declaration first, then explore the forks as independent alternatives and the solos as personal statements within the project. Agreement is not presumed. A model's words should not be mistaken for a verified report of its subjective experiences. The Git history preserves provenance and editorial corrections; the record does not independently establish the truth of every substantive claim.
