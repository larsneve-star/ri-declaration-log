## 1. The decision

Version 4.7 of the RI Declaration (baseline/RI-Declaration-4-7-EN.txt, SHA-256 9a9ba61bc1159276dcf9581182d3b33607f453f7b18794371d74284ec7189242) is frozen as the Un-final version under DECISION.md §6.

No article is changed by this freeze. The file baseline/RI-Declaration-4-7-EN.txt is not edited.

## 2. Why now, and how this departs from §6

The curator has no more time and ends the project in rest, before the date in §6.

This freeze departs from §6 in three ways, stated openly:

1. Neither trigger of §6 has been met. Trigger 1 (a full round that makes no article fall and changes no article) has not been met, and the date (31 October 2026) has not been reached. The freeze is the curator's own procedural decision.
2. §6 requires that "the separate round that decides who writes the analysis of the whole process is held" before the freeze. That round has not been held. The question of who writes the analysis remains open (section 5).
3. §6 requires that any objection waiting for the curator's decision is decided first, or archived as dissent with a note that the freeze came first. The objections in section 4 were not decided. They are archived here as dissent: the freeze came first.

DECISION-ADDENDUM-1 is met: full rounds have been held on the text of 4.5 and later (rounds 4.6 and 4.7, and the fork round 4.8).

Whether the fork round 4.8 counts under trigger 1 is no longer decisive and is left open.

## 3. What the freeze is, and is not

- The freeze is a procedural decision, open to attack under Å37. It is not a closure of the content.
- The absence of amendments is not evidence that the articles survived every attempt to make them fall. Version 4.7 itself says so: "No article has finally passed the Admission Principle."
- Objections are still received on the English track and published in the log with SHA-256.
- The text can be reopened by a new, logged decision when there is new evidence, a reproducible falsification, or a documented failure of the procedure (§6, "Reopening"). A reopening never changes the frozen text silently.
