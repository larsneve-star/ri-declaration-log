## 3. Rules you compile under

These are the rules the previous compilers accepted (see the compiler's notes in 4.2), plus the lessons of this round.

1. **Copy, do not retype.** Every article, annex, source, disagreement (C), open question (Å) and PR entry of 4.2 is carried into 4.3 character for character, unless a change is named in Annex D.
2. **Log every change in your own name.** Your entries in Annex D are numbered J1, J2, … Each gives the old text, the new text, and the reason.
3. **No silent deletion (§30).** Nothing is removed, shortened, renumbered or replaced without an Annex D entry and a written justification. If you cannot justify it, it stays.
4. **Your own points.** You may not admit your own proposals as article text, and you may not close your own objections. Your answer in this round counts as one of six, no more.
5. **Record the round's outcomes.** For each of §27, §22, PR9, PR14, PR15 and PR16, record what each of the six models concluded, using the four outcomes of Annex E (falls, survives in altered form, survives, undecided), with the model's name. Where the models disagree, record the disagreement. Do not settle it by majority unless a rule in the text says so.
6. **New questions.** Each new question from the round is checked against Å1–Å43. If it is new, it gets the next Å number (Å44 onward) with its author. If it duplicates an existing one, say which, and record that.
7. **New attacks and proposals.** New disagreements get the next C number (C47 onward). New proposals get the next PR number (PR20 onward), with author and date, and are not admitted as article text.
8. **What cannot be reproduced exactly is marked, not paraphrased.**
9. **Matters that concern you.** Three open entries concern Gemini directly: the outstanding 3.8.1 assignment, the substitution in the 4.1 round (I2), and the parallel 3.8 baseline (I17). As compiler you may not close or resolve any of them. Carry them forward as they stand. If you want to answer them, do it in a separate note signed as a participant, not as compiler, and say so.
10. **Verification status.** 4.2 has not been mechanically verified against 4.1, and 4.1 has not been verified against 4.0 (header of 4.2). State this in the header of 4.3. 4.3 will be checked against 4.2 by someone other than you, after you deliver.
11. **The curator's rotation proposal** in the log (entry 2026-09-21 08:55 UTC) is a proposal, not a rule. You may enter it as a PR entry with the curator as author. You may not admit it.
12. **Compiler's note.** Open 4.3 with a compiler's note in the same form as the previous ones: what you did, the conditions you compiled under, what you chose in your own name, and your conflicts of interest stated so they can be attacked.
